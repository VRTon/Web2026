Step by step

Make your contribution count

Read these steps before donating and keep your receipt.

  1. 1

    Follow the official link

    During the donation period, use the button on this page to access Banco de Chile's official donation portal for Teletón.

  2. 2

    Complete your donation

    If you are donating from outside Chile, select “aporte desde el extranjero” (donate from abroad) and fill in the requested details. Check that your card supports international payments in US dollars.

  3. 3

    Send your receipt

    Take a photo or screenshot of your receipt, redact your personal information and send it to the “Donaciones” channel on our Discord to add your contribution to VRTon's total.

Redact your details before sharing

Hide your full name, RUT or identity document, email, address, account or card numbers and any other personal information. Never share passwords or security codes.

Leave the amount, currency, date and donation confirmation visible so the team can count your contribution.

Send your receipt on Discord ↗

How donations are counted

A community total

VRTon adds up the amounts on receipts submitted to the “Donaciones” Discord channel to track the community's total contributions. Donating through the official portal does not automatically add your contribution to VRTon's count: remember to submit your receipt once.

This count is based on the receipts received. It is not a bank certification of settled funds and does not mean that money passes through VRTon. If you donated during the donation period and still need to submit your receipt, you can do so on Discord even after the period closes.

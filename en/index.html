About VRTon

VRTon in a nutshell

Virtual communities, real impact

VRTon is a volunteer-driven initiative that brings together people and communities from different parts of the world around a shared passion: technology, virtual reality, and its ability to connect people.

We use virtual reality, primarily through VRChat, as a space to promote solidarity, educate and raise awareness about different realities, strengthen connections between communities, and raise funds to create a positive and tangible impact.

Attend

In support of Teletón Chile

For the fourth consecutive year, VRTon supports Teletón Chile, a nonprofit institution that provides free comprehensive rehabilitation to children and young people with motor disabilities and promotes their social inclusion. Donations are made only through Teletón's official channels.

Useful information

Frequently asked questions

What is VRTon? +
VRTon is a voluntary initiative that brings together communities from the virtual reality metaverse to achieve solidarity goals. We focus on education, inspiration, empathy, and philanthropy while using technology to help others and improve lives.
How can I attend an event? +
This year's event will be held on the 6th and 7th of November. You can attend by joining our VRChat Group. The event is completely free.
Do I need VR equipment to participate? +
No! While VR headsets provide the most immersive experience, you can join our events using a computer or even your phone!
How can I volunteer? +
We're always looking for passionate individuals to join our team! Click here to learn more about the volunteer opportunities available.
Where does the money go? +
100% of the funds raised go directly to our partner charities and causes. We promote donating using the official links provided by the organizations and don't collect money ourselves.
What kind of activities can we expect on VRTon? +
You can expect a variety of activities on VRTon catering to different interests and age groups, including live concerts and shows, educational workshops, awareness campaigns, and community gatherings.
How can I stay updated on upcoming events? +
Follow us on our social media channels (Discord, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, and Twitch) for the latest news, event announcements, and behind-the-scenes content.

VRTon 2026 Terms and Conditions

1. Scope

These Terms and Conditions apply to the use of all spaces and activities related to VRTon, including the main event, Discord server, VRChat group, official website, and social media. They apply before, during, and after the event.

2. Compliance with External Standards

All participants must respect the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines of the platforms used:

  • VRChat - Terms of Service

  • Discord - Terms of Service

3. Access and Eligibility

  • Access to the event is restricted to people over 18 years of age, except in cases expressly pre-approved by the management team and with authorization from a legal guardian.
  • Entry to the event is free.
  • There are no geographical restrictions to participate.
  • Access to the event in VRChat does not require a virtual reality headset, although the platform may impose its own technical or access restrictions.

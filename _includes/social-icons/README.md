# Social icons

Discord, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube and Twitch SVGs are from
[Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons),
downloaded on 2026-11-08. Simple Icons distributes these assets under
[CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners.

The SVGs are included inline and inherit `currentColor` from `.social-icon`.
Links provide accessible names; the icon wrapper is hidden from assistive technology.

VRTon is built by volunteers who contribute art, engineering, production, moderation, communication, and community support. This guide describes how we collaborate; it is not a substitute for the detailed [Terms of Service](/legal/terms/).

## Joining and working with a team

- **Volunteering is unpaid**, unless a separate written paid engagement has been agreed.
- Choose responsibilities that fit your availability and abilities. Keep your leader informed of important progress, delays, and absences.
- Normally, join **up to two teams**. Leaders are appointed by the Organizing Team, and one person can lead **one team at a time**.
- Discord work channels are the main coordination space. Some work sessions or meetings may be recorded with appropriate notice.
- You may withdraw; when practical, notify your lead and arrange a reasonable handover. Respect confidential information, other members, and official staff permissions.
- If you have a concern involving your leader or another organizer, contact a different authorized organizer or **[support@vrton.org](mailto:support@vrton.org)**.

[Terms Articles 5, 13–14](/legal/terms/#article-13-volunteers-status-and-participation)

## Creative work and recognition

- **You remain the original author** of original work you create, unless a separate written agreement provides otherwise.
- When your contribution is accepted for VRTon, the authorized organizers receive the extensive, lasting license described in the Terms. They can keep, edit, maintain, and reuse it across VRTon editions after you leave.
- **Do not sell, redistribute, or relicense VRTon-branded assets** for independent projects without written permission, even when you created the original artwork or model. Reusable unbranded components may be different, subject to third-party rights and any separate agreement.
- Approved portfolio use and fair attribution are encouraged. Please respect unreleased material, confidential files, and purchased or third-party asset licenses. You may request correction or omission of public credit.

[Terms Article 15 — Contributions](/legal/terms/#article-15-contributions-creator-attribution-and-licenses) · [Article 16 — Branding](/legal/terms/#article-16-vrton-and-partner-trademarks-identities-and-publicity)

## Partner communities

Participating communities can contribute activities, promotion, or prizes, and may independently organize fundraising. They coordinate through an agreed representative and use the VRTon identity **only to promote the initiative and the edition/activity in which they participate**.

**VRTon never handles donation funds.** Independent partners control and are responsible for their own collections; they must never claim to collect *on behalf of VRTon*. Only donation links expressly identified by VRTon as the charity's official channels are VRTon-endorsed.

VRTon may use the partner's own logo in materials connected to the edition or activity in which that partner participates, but not in unrelated future edition advertising without new permission.

[Terms Articles 11 and 16](/legal/terms/#article-11-partner-fundraising-official-channels-and-non-endorsement)

## Performing at VRTon

Artists and DJs with a scheduled slot should read the dedicated [Performer Guidelines](/legal/performer-guidelines/). Being a guest performer does not automatically make someone a standing team volunteer. Everyone remains subject to the [Code of Conduct](/legal/code-of-conduct/).

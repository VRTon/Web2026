This guide summarizes collaboration requirements. The [Terms of Service](/legal/terms/) govern participation, roles, contributions, and permissions.

## Joining and working with a team

- Ordinary volunteer roles are **18+ and unpaid**, unless a separate written paid engagement is agreed. Mandatory employment protections remain applicable.
- Organizers determine team structure, appointments, responsibilities, and access. Accept commitments you can meet and promptly report delays or absences to your designated contact.
- Follow authorized instructions and protect confidential materials and personal data. Work sessions may be recorded with appropriate notice.
- You may leave; VRTon may reassign or end roles under the Terms. Arrange a reasonable handover where practical.
- For concerns involving your contact, use another authorized organizer or **[support@vrton.org](mailto:support@vrton.org)**.

[Terms Article 13 — Participation](/legal/terms/#article-13-volunteers-status-and-participation) · [Article 14 — Activity commitments](/legal/terms/#article-14-teams-leadership-and-activity-commitments)

## Creative work and recognition

You retain ownership of your original work unless otherwise agreed. Accepted contributions carry the **lasting license in Article 15**, including reuse and adaptation across editions after you leave. Disclose third-party licenses and restrictions before submitting work.

Do not independently sell, redistribute, or relicense VRTon-branded assets without the relevant written permissions. Portfolio use must respect confidentiality, publication schedules, branding, and third-party rights. Credit format and placement are discretionary except where legally or contractually required; corrections or omission may be requested.

[Terms Article 15 — Contributions](/legal/terms/#article-15-contributions-creator-attribution-and-licenses) · [Article 16 — Branding](/legal/terms/#article-16-vrton-and-partner-trademarks-identities-and-publicity)

## Partner communities

Coordinate the agreed activity and use of identities with authorized organizers. A collaboration does not grant general authority to represent VRTon or imply endorsement of unrelated activities.

**VRTon does not handle donation funds.** Partners are responsible for independent collections and must not claim to collect on VRTon's behalf. Use only expressly identified official channels for donations to the beneficiary. Any raffle or prize activity requires its own lawful published rules.

[Terms Article 11 — Partner fundraising](/legal/terms/#article-11-partner-fundraising-official-channels-and-non-endorsement) · [Article 12 — Prizes](/legal/terms/#article-12-solidarity-raffle-and-donated-prizes)

## Performing at VRTon

Scheduled artists must also follow the [Performer Guidelines](/legal/performer-guidelines/). A guest appearance does not create a standing volunteer role. The [Code of Conduct](/legal/code-of-conduct/) applies to everyone.

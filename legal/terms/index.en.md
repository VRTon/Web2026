> **Participation is subject to these Terms and applicable platform rules.** Official event spaces may be broadcast or recorded. VRTon may restrict participation and change activities as described below. Donations go to the beneficiary, not to VRTon.

## Article 1: Organizer, mission, and definitions {#article-1-organizer-mission-and-definitions}

VRTon is an independent community initiative coordinated by its Organizing Team. In these Terms, **VRTon** means the authorized organizers responsible for the relevant activity; **Staff** means personnel authorized to act for it. VRTon is separate from its charitable beneficiaries and partner communities. No participant may bind or represent VRTon without authorization.

**Official Spaces** are event instances, groups, channels, broadcasts, and services operated or moderated by VRTon. These Terms cover attendees, volunteers, performers, and collaborating communities before, during, and after an edition.

## Article 2: Scope, notice, acceptance, and document hierarchy {#article-2-scope-notice-acceptance-and-document-hierarchy}

By joining an official activity after these Terms have been made available to you, you agree to follow them to the extent permitted by law. If you do not agree, do not participate. Merely viewing a public website or broadcast does not by itself establish acceptance of every contractual provision.

The [Code of Conduct](/legal/code-of-conduct/), [Volunteering Guide](/legal/volunteering/), and [Performer Guidelines](/legal/performer-guidelines/) explain the applicable participation requirements. These Terms prevail over conflicting summaries. Specific written agreements govern their subject matter; the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/) governs information about personal-data handling. Additional material conditions must be communicated before acceptance. External platform rules apply independently.

## Article 3: Access, age, and participation {#article-3-access-age-and-participation}

1. Official VRChat events, the main Discord community, and volunteer roles are **18+**. Organizers may approve a supervised institutional visit for ages **13–17** in a designated non-age-gated instance. The institution must arrange guardian permissions, continuous in-person supervision, and any required recording permissions. The exception applies only to the approved session; it grants no access to adult-only services. Platform age, individual-account, and verification requirements always apply.
2. Ordinary event admission is free and does not require a donation. Participants supply their own compatible equipment and accounts. Public streams and websites follow the viewing platform's eligibility rules.
3. Admission, roles, and capacity are subject to availability and the safety, conduct, technical, and operational requirements of the activity. Approval of an application, invitation, or previous attendance does not guarantee continued access or selection for future editions.

## Article 4: Platform compliance and official spaces {#article-4-platform-compliance-and-official-spaces}

Follow applicable law, platform rules, and Staff instructions concerning participation, content, equipment, and event operations. Relevant rules include the [VRChat Terms](https://hello.vrchat.com/legal), [VRChat Community Guidelines](https://hello.vrchat.com/community-guidelines), [Discord Terms](https://discord.com/terms), and [Discord Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

VRTon may consider conduct outside Official Spaces when it has a relevant connection to participant safety, fraud, harassment, event disruption, or misuse of VRTon's identity. VRTon does not control independent platforms or communities.

## Article 5: Event broadcasts, recording, and media use {#article-5-event-broadcasts-recording-and-media-use}

1. **Official event instances may be livestreamed, photographed, or recorded at any time; no area is guaranteed to be off-camera.** Captures may include avatars, names, voices, performances, visible messages, and official stream chats. Work sessions and meetings may also be recorded with appropriate notice. Ordinary social channels and private messages are outside routine event recording, without preventing lawful preservation of incident evidence.
2. To the extent you hold the relevant rights and validly agree after notice, you authorize the organizers and their production collaborators to capture, edit, publish, broadcast, archive, and reuse your event appearance and contributions worldwide for VRTon documentation, publicity, education, and fundraising, without appearance fees. This authorization does not replace separate consent or another legal basis where required, including for identifiable minors. Additional appearance agreements may apply.
3. Inclusion, continued availability, and particular edits are not guaranteed. Media requests follow the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/). VRTon cannot undo a live broadcast or control independently saved third-party copies. Third-party rights remain subject to Articles 16–17.

## Article 6: Respect, safety, and prohibited content {#article-6-respect-safety-and-prohibited-content}

The following are prohibited in conduct, avatars, profiles, messages, and other shared content:

- Sexual activity, explicit nudity, erotic roleplay, extreme gore, and content encouraging self-harm. Official activities remain SFW despite their adult admission rule.
- Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, stalking, coercion, unwanted sexual advances, and repeated unwanted contact.
- Political campaigning, religious proselytizing, and disruptive disputes during activities.
- Impersonation, fabricated evidence, knowingly false accusations, fraud, malicious links, and unauthorized disclosure of private information.
- Spam, unauthorized advertising, fundraising links, and deliberate diversion or disruption of activities.
- Pirated material, malicious clients, crashers, harmful exploits, intrusive effects, and interference with participants, streams, or systems.

These examples are not exhaustive. Staff may address comparable harmful or disruptive conduct under Article 8. Good-faith complaints and lawful reporting are permitted; this does not excuse harassment or disruption in how they are made.

## Article 7: Avatars, accessibility, and media selection {#article-7-avatars-accessibility-and-media-selection}

Use an optimized avatar suitable for the activity. Staff may require an avatar change, apply technical limits, or hide avatars for safety, performance, accessibility, or production needs. Permission to attend does not guarantee appearance in recordings. Performer exceptions require production approval.

## Article 8: Moderation measures and appeals {#article-8-moderation-measures-and-appeals}

1. **VRTon retains discretion over participation in its spaces**, exercised in good faith and subject to applicable law. Staff may remove content, warn, mute, restrict, remove, suspend, or ban participants; revoke roles or access; and refer incidents to platforms or authorities. Grounds include actual or reasonably suspected rule breaches, safety or security risks, disruption, and operational needs. Temporary restrictions may be used while concerns are assessed.
2. Measures need not follow a fixed sequence of warnings or sanctions. Immediate action may be taken where warranted, without completing an investigation first. Decisions may take account of the context, available information, severity, and prior conduct. VRTon does not undertake continuous monitoring or guarantee detection of every incident.
3. You may request review through the designated Discord process or **[support@vrton.org](mailto:support@vrton.org)** if Discord is unavailable. Identify the account, action, and relevant grounds. A request does not suspend the measure or guarantee reversal. VRTon may close repetitive or abusive requests that add no material information, subject to any legally required review procedure. Do not contact individual moderators by private message.
4. VRTon may withhold confidential evidence, reporter identities, and internal security or deliberative material, except where disclosure is legally required. Nothing in this process limits statutory information rights, complaints to competent authorities, or access to legal remedies.

## Article 9: Reporting safety concerns and privacy of reports {#article-9-reporting-safety-concerns-and-privacy-of-reports}

Use the designated private reporting route or **[support@vrton.org](mailto:support@vrton.org)**. Provide relevant facts and evidence you are lawfully entitled to share; do not publish sensitive allegations or personal records in public channels. Reports and evidence may be used or shared as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/). Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised where lawful disclosure is necessary.

## Article 10: Donations, proof, and published totals {#article-10-donations-proof-and-published-totals}

1. **VRTon does not collect, hold, process, or transfer donated money.** Use only the beneficiary's donation channels identified by VRTon as official. Payment processing, receipts, disputes, and refunds are handled by the beneficiary or payment provider.
2. VRTon's totals are based on submitted donation vouchers, **not independently verified payment settlements**. Totals are provisional and may be corrected for duplication, invalid records, currency conversion, or other errors. VRTon may request clarification or exclude unverifiable submissions. Do not submit misleading or fraudulent vouchers.
3. Submission requirements and any material conversion assumptions are provided through the relevant donation or reporting channel. Public anonymity and personal-data handling follow the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/). Avoid including unrelated sensitive information in vouchers.

## Article 11: Partner fundraising, official channels, and non-endorsement {#article-11-partner-fundraising-official-channels-and-non-endorsement}

Partner communities run their own collections and are responsible for their funds, representations, accounting, and obligations. They must not claim to receive money on VRTon's behalf. Collaboration does not make a partner's payment link an official or guaranteed donation channel.

Permission to use VRTon's identity is limited to the authorized collaboration, is non-transferable, and may be withdrawn for misuse. It does not authorize unrelated sales, false endorsement, or commitments on VRTon's behalf.

## Article 12: Solidarity Raffle and donated prizes {#article-12-solidarity-raffle-and-donated-prizes}

Any raffle or prize draw is a separate, optional activity that may proceed **only where lawful and under its published specific rules and required authorizations**. Those rules must identify eligibility, entry and selection methods, prizes, deadlines, and claim conditions before entries open. A donation alone creates no entitlement to tickets or prizes outside a lawful published promotion.

VRTon may suspend, modify, or cancel a promotion for legal, safety, fraud, or operational reasons, subject to its published rules and mandatory participant rights. Prize providers must hold the necessary rights and fulfill their accepted commitments. These Terms do not authorize a raffle or certify its legality in any jurisdiction.

## Article 13: Volunteers, status, and participation {#article-13-volunteers-status-and-participation}

Volunteering is optional and unpaid unless a separate written paid engagement is agreed. It does not itself promise employment, payment, or a continuing role; any mandatory employment protections remain applicable.

Volunteers must fulfill accepted responsibilities, follow authorized instructions, and protect confidential materials and personal information. Volunteers may leave, and VRTon may reassign or end roles and access for conduct, suitability, availability, or organizational reasons, subject to applicable law and any separate agreement. Coordinate a reasonable handover where practical.

## Article 14: Teams, leadership, and activity commitments {#article-14-teams-leadership-and-activity-commitments}

Organizers determine team structure, appointments, access permissions, and scheduling. Participants must follow the responsibilities, deadlines, and technical requirements communicated for their activity and promptly report inability to meet a commitment.

Scheduled artists must also follow the [Performer Guidelines](/legal/performer-guidelines/) and their confirmed brief. VRTon may change, interrupt, replace, or cancel an activity for safety, rights, technical, or production reasons, subject to any separate agreement. Acceptance does not guarantee a particular audience, publicity, recording, or future booking.

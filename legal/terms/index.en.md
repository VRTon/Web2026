> **Please read this document before participating.** VRTon is an independent, community-driven charitable event operated under a trademark registered in Chile. Official VRChat events are generally intended for adults, with a narrow supervised institutional exception described in Article 3. Official activities may be broadcast or recorded, and authorized staff moderate VRTon spaces. VRTon does not receive donated funds or independently verify payments with payment processors.

## Article 1: Organizer, mission, and definitions {#article-1-organizer-mission-and-definitions}

1. **VRTon** is an independent, community-driven charitable event organized by its authorized coordinating team (the **Organizing Team**) and operating under the **VRTon trademark registered in Chile**. References in these Terms to actions, permissions, licenses, and decisions by “VRTon” mean the relevant authorized organizers or legal rights holders acting for the initiative. The VRTon brand, its organizers, and the charitable beneficiary are distinct from one another; a trademark registration does not itself establish a separate corporate entity.
2. VRTon has an **official collaboration** with Teletón Chile for relevant editions. VRTon and Teletón Chile remain **separate initiatives/entities**: VRTon is not a department, subsidiary, or legal representative of Teletón Chile. No participant, volunteer, or partner may imply otherwise.
3. A **Participant** is anyone who takes part in a covered official event or activity, including through VRChat, the official Discord server, designated livestream chats, performances, or volunteer work. A **Volunteer** assists without agreed financial compensation; a **Partner Community** is an independently operated group collaborating with VRTon; **Staff** means authorized moderators, organizers, or personnel acting within assigned roles.
4. **Official Spaces** include VRTon-operated event instances and groups in VRChat, official Discord channels and activities, the VRTon website and VRTon-controlled communications, and official VRTon broadcasts and associated chats to the extent VRTon can moderate them. Unaffiliated third-party groups, websites, payment accounts, and private conversations are not automatically Official Spaces.
5. **Official Donation Channels** are the donation destinations specifically identified or linked by VRTon as belonging to the selected charitable beneficiary. An independent partner's payment account is not an Official Donation Channel merely because the partner collaborates with VRTon.
6. The applicable VRTon edition and its schedules, channels, approved partnerships, prize rules, and beneficiary will be announced through official communications. Terms in this document apply to successive editions unless a duly published edition-specific rule or separate signed agreement provides otherwise, subject to applicable law.

## Article 3: Access, age, and participation {#article-3-access-age-and-participation}

1. **General admission:** Official VRTon VRChat event instances, the official VRTon Discord community, and volunteer roles are generally intended for persons **18 years of age or older**. VRTon may restrict admission accordingly. No special VRTon permission permits access to an age-gated instance without the platform-required age verification.
2. **Limited supervised institutional exception:** The Organizing Team may preapprove participants **aged 13–17** to experience a scheduled segment of the event in a **designated non-age-gated main VRChat instance**, as part of a formally coordinated institutional activity. Approval requires documented permission from a parent or legal guardian, continuous supervision by the responsible institution in person, supervision by VRTon moderators in the instance, and compliance with applicable platform rules and protective procedures. Permission is limited to the approved institutional session; it does not confer ordinary Discord membership, volunteer eligibility, access to any age-gated instances, or permission to participate in adult-only activities. VRTon may refuse, limit, or revoke an exception when necessary for safety or compliance.
3. **Accounts and equipment:** Institutions may arrange use of shared physical VR equipment, but must ensure **each VRChat account is used only by its individual account holder and has that user’s accurate date of birth**, unless VRChat itself expressly authorizes a different arrangement. VRTon cannot authorize account sharing or inaccurate ages contrary to platform rules.
4. **Minors in broadcasts:** The institution must obtain appropriate guardian permissions for the supervised session and any identifiable appearance in livestreams or recorded media, supply any additional notices or permissions legally required, and coordinate safeguards with VRTon. Where required, VRTon will seek separate permission for specific media uses. An ordinary event notice alone is not treated as universal consent for a minor.
5. Publicly accessible websites and livestreams may be viewed by individuals who meet the requirements of the applicable viewing platform. Public viewing does not grant access to restricted communities or event instances.
6. Access to VRTon-run event instances is **free**; a donation is never required for ordinary admission. No VR headset is required where VRChat supports desktop access, but users are responsible for meeting the platform’s technical requirements.
7. VRTon does not generally impose geographic eligibility restrictions on ordinary participation, but particular platforms, activities, promotions, or laws may impose territorial or other limits. Prize-draw eligibility is addressed in Article 12.
8. Access, roles, instance capacity, and participation may be limited for safety, platform compliance, age verification, technical constraints, or moderation reasons. VRTon may reschedule, alter, or end activities as reasonably necessary.

## Article 5: Event broadcasts, recording, and media use {#article-5-event-broadcasts-recording-and-media-use}

1. **Any part of an official VRTon event or activity may be livestreamed, photographed, captured, or recorded at any time. No location within an official VRChat event instance is guaranteed to be off-camera.** Avatars, screen/display names, voices, text in captured event views, gestures, performances, and participant interactions may be included.
2. Official Discord **volunteer work channels, organized work sessions, and meetings** may also be recorded or used in authorized event-related media; participants should receive appropriate channel- or meeting-level notice. **Discord direct messages and ordinary general/social channels are outside VRTon's routine recording scope.** Those exclusions do not prohibit lawful and proportionate preservation of evidence in a specific reported safety incident.
3. Contributions visible in official **Twitch or YouTube chats**, and other official event-related broadcast interactions, may appear live or in recordings, screenshots, highlights, or promotional recaps. Livestream platforms may separately collect and process viewer information under their own policies.
4. With appropriate notice, participants acknowledge these capture practices and, to the extent they can lawfully grant permission, authorize the VRTon organizers and authorized production collaborators to record and use their **event appearance and contributions** for broadcasting, editing, captioning, highlights, archives, news about VRTon, documentation, educational presentations, fundraising communication, and future VRTon-branded promotion worldwide, without individual appearance fees. Such permission is subject to non-waivable image, voice, privacy, copyright, and other personal rights. **Separate explicit permission will be sought where the law or the nature of the proposed use requires it.**
5. Publication on official VRTon media does not grant unrestricted rights to others. VRTon-branded media may be reused for VRTon-related purposes, but third-party content and trademarks remain subject to their separate rights and permissions under Articles 16–17.
6. No participant is guaranteed inclusion in a live broadcast, photo, or edited material. To avoid routine event recording, do not enter or interact in covered broadcast areas or activities. Private attendance exceptions cannot be guaranteed.
7. Participants can request review, correction, removal, or restriction of identifiable published material by emailing **support@vrton.org**. Requests will be evaluated under applicable law, the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/), third-party rights, and technical feasibility. A live broadcast cannot be undone, and VRTon cannot guarantee the removal of copies independently saved or redistributed by third parties.
8. Featured performers, interviewees, presenters, and commissioned participants may be asked to sign separate appearance and rights agreements.

## Article 6: Respect, safety, and prohibited content {#article-6-respect-safety-and-prohibited-content}

These restrictions apply to speech and behavior and to avatars, profile images, names, banners, emojis, stickers, effects, animations, audio, shared links, and other digital content in Official Spaces.

1. **SFW conduct:** No visible explicit nudity, sexual activity or simulated sexual activity, erotic roleplay (ERP), explicit sexual gestures, or sexually explicit profile/media content. Staff may ask for changes to borderline material; an 18+ entry rule does **not** make official spaces NSFW.
2. **Graphic and harmful material:** No extreme gore, graphic violence, gratuitously disturbing imagery, or content encouraging or glorifying suicide or self-harm. Good-faith, non-graphic requests for support are not prohibited by this rule.
3. **Discrimination and harassment:** No hateful, racist, xenophobic, homophobic, transphobic, sexist, or otherwise discriminatory abuse; bullying, threats, stalking, coercion, unwanted sexual advances, repeated unwanted contact, or deliberate invasion of personal boundaries. Respect users' refusals, blocks, and personal-space limits.
4. **Campaigning and disruptive disputes:** VRTon is nonpartisan and nonsectarian. Political campaigning, religious proselytizing, and persistently confrontational or disruptive political/religious disputes are not permitted during official activities. Respectful incidental references to personal beliefs or culture are not automatically violations.
5. **Honest concerns versus sabotage:** Respectful criticism, factual questions about VRTon or a charity, and good-faith reports are permitted. **Knowingly false allegations, fabricated evidence, impersonation, coordinated disinformation, intimidation, malicious rumor campaigns, or repeated disruptive accusations intended to harass or sabotage** are prohibited. Staff must assess context and evidence rather than treating criticism alone as a violation. No retaliation for good-faith reporting.
6. **Spam and diversion:** No repeated unsolicited messages, unauthorized advertising or promotional links, unapproved invitations to external communities or commercial services, spam group invitations, unauthorized fundraising links, or VRChat portals deliberately used to divert participants from VRTon activities.
7. **Privacy and security:** No doxxing, publication of another person's nonpublic personal details without authorization, sharing of private credentials or screenshots, phishing, scams, fraudulent charity links, account theft, or malicious downloads. Avoid publicly sharing your own sensitive financial or identity details.
8. **Unauthorized content and illegal behavior:** No distribution of pirated, ripped, or otherwise unlawfully obtained avatars, assets, worlds, music, or other media. No unlawful activities or instructions designed to enable them.
9. **Disruption and exploitation:** No voice spam, intentional interruption of presentations, malicious clients, crashing tools, harmful exploits, abusive particles, intrusive shaders, disruptive sounds, or attempts to interfere with streams, instances, staff systems, or event operations.
10. These examples are not exhaustive. Staff will apply the standards reasonably, proportionately, and consistently in light of the circumstances and applicable platform rules.

## Article 7: Avatars, accessibility, and media selection {#article-7-avatars-accessibility-and-media-selection}

1. VRTon **recommends optimized avatars** to support accessibility, comfort, streaming, and performance for everyone. Where available, VRTon provides public optimized avatars for participants who wish to use one.
2. An avatar that is compliant with Article 6 is **not automatically prohibited solely because it is unoptimized**. However, VRTon **does not guarantee** that an unoptimized avatar will appear in livestreams, photos, recordings, or other media; production staff may hide or filter avatars for performance and presentation reasons.
3. Staff may require changes to an avatar whose behavior or performance seriously disrupts other users or the event. Malicious crashers, exploits, and intentionally disruptive effects are subject to Article 8.
4. Published avatar optimization recommendations are operational guidance, not a promise of appearance or endorsement of particular tools.

## Article 8: Moderation measures and appeals {#article-8-moderation-measures-and-appeals}

1. Authorized moderation staff may issue reminders and warnings; request an avatar or conduct change; mute, restrict, remove, suspend, or ban accounts from VRTon-controlled spaces; revoke participation privileges; or refer conduct to the relevant platform. Actions must be proportionate to the reasonably assessed safety and operational risk.
2. VRTon may act **immediately without a prior warning** where reasonably necessary to protect users, recordings, infrastructure, or the event. Moderation in VRTon spaces is separate from actions imposed by VRChat, Discord, Twitch, or YouTube.
3. **All VRTon moderation actions may be appealed** through the designated appeal process in the official VRTon Discord server. Anyone banned from that Discord server may instead submit an appeal by email to **support@vrton.org**. **Do not direct-message individual moderation team members to file an appeal.**
4. Appeals should identify the affected account, contested action, approximate date, and any relevant explanation or evidence. VRTon may request information reasonably necessary to assess the matter. Submitting an appeal does not automatically suspend a sanction or guarantee reversal.
5. VRTon aims to explain the general reason for substantial actions when reasonably possible, but may withhold private reporter identities, sensitive evidence, internal security methods, and information whose disclosure could endanger others or impair an investigation.
6. VRTon will not penalize someone merely for making a good-faith appeal or report. Abuse of the appeals process, threats, fabricated evidence, or persistent harassment can be addressed separately.

## Article 9: Reporting safety concerns and privacy of reports {#article-9-reporting-safety-concerns-and-privacy-of-reports}

1. Reports should be sent through authorized private reporting channels announced in the official Discord server or to **support@vrton.org**. Do not expose sensitive allegations, donation vouchers, or another person's private information in public channels.
2. Helpful reports may include platform/user identifiers, the time and location, a factual description, and relevant screenshots, links, or recordings you are lawfully entitled to share. Do not obtain evidence through unauthorized account access or other unlawful means.
3. Moderation reports are accessible to the moderation personnel handling them, with access extended to authorized organizers or advisers only where reasonably necessary. VRTon may disclose information where required by law or for an appropriate platform safety report.
4. Reports are evaluated on available evidence. Confidentiality is handled with care but cannot be absolutely guaranteed where disclosure is legally required or necessary for a fair and safe response. Retaliation for good-faith reports is prohibited.

## Article 10: Donations, proof, and published totals {#article-10-donations-proof-and-published-totals}

1. **VRTon never collects, receives, holds, processes, or transfers donated money directly.** Donations intended for the selected charity should be made through its **Official Donation Channels**. Payment disputes, receipts, refunds, chargebacks, and processing questions are handled by the relevant beneficiary and payment provider under their terms.
2. **VRTon has no payment-processor integration, payment feed, or direct verification arrangement.** VRTon's fundraising figures are compiled from **donation vouchers submitted by individuals or partner communities**, and must not be described as independently confirmed settlements to the charity.
3. Participants submit vouchers through the designated **Discord donation bot in the specified channel**. The submission process requests the voucher image, amount, currency, donation date/time, and submitting user's identity. Submitters may choose **public anonymity** in the bot; this does not remove the submitting account identifier from VRTon's internal record. Details of processing appear in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/).
4. Voucher submitters are **advised to obscure** account numbers, addresses, identity-document numbers, and unrelated transaction details before uploading. Redaction is recommended rather than a mandatory condition of submission, but VRTon may ask for a safer replacement or refuse to publish unnecessarily sensitive content.
5. **Only the Directive/Organizing Team responsible for donation verification may access submitted donation vouchers and associated internal records**, except limited access by necessary service providers or where legally required. Public recognition of donor identities or individual amounts occurs **only where the donor has not requested anonymity and the disclosure has been appropriately indicated or authorized**. Public totals may be displayed in aggregate.
6. Staff review submitted vouchers manually for apparent plausibility, completeness, and potential duplication. During an event, **displayed totals are provisional and assume submitted vouchers are valid**. VRTon may reconcile, correct, or republish those totals after the event when errors, duplicates, invalid submissions, exchange-rate questions, or new information arise.
7. VRTon cannot guarantee that every submitted voucher corresponds to a completed, unreversed donation; it cannot independently confirm funds arrived at the beneficiary. Knowingly submitting forged, duplicated-as-new, altered-to-mislead, or otherwise fraudulent vouchers is prohibited.
8. Any currency-conversion assumptions used in public totals will be explained with the relevant reporting figures where material. Public statements should clearly differentiate voucher-reported totals from any totals separately confirmed by the beneficiary.

2. Independent campaigns must not claim to collect **on behalf of VRTon**, falsely suggest they are Official Donation Channels, or represent VRTon as the payment recipient. Any fund transfers arranged by partners are entirely separate from VRTon's receipt-based public counting function.
3. **VRTon does not endorse or guarantee any donation URL other than the Official Donation Channels it expressly identifies.** A partner's page, wallet, processor link, or collection box is not a VRTon-endorsed payment method simply because the community participates in the event.
4. Approved collaborating communities may use authorized VRTon names and marks **solely to promote the charitable initiative and the edition/activity in which they participate**, following published brand rules. This permission is limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, and revocable for misuse; it does not permit fundraising impersonation, false representation, merchandise sales, or unrelated promotions.
5. Partner communities are responsible for their own representations, accounting, participant disclosures, legality, payment services, and promises. Nothing in this section removes responsibilities imposed on VRTon by mandatory law for its own acts.

## Article 12: Solidarity Raffle and donated prizes {#article-12-solidarity-raffle-and-donated-prizes}

1. VRTon may operate a **Solidarity Raffle** as a special event activity **only where legally permitted and after any necessary approvals and activity-specific rules are in place**. Participation in a donation-related prize draw does not change Article 10: donated money is paid to the beneficiary, not to VRTon.
2. Subject to the published raffle rules and eligibility verification, a qualifying **individual, non-anonymous donation** receives **one ticket for each complete US$5 donated (or its published equivalent in another currency)**. Fractions below a complete US$5 do not produce additional tickets. Donation vouchers may be aggregated for an identified eligible individual if accepted under the announced raffle administration rules.
3. **Anonymous donations and community/group donations are not eligible for raffle tickets.** Choosing anonymity in the donation submission process therefore excludes that submission from ticket allocation, even though VRTon may have an internal account identifier for bookkeeping.
4. Ticket allocation depends on sufficient identifying information, submission of an acceptable voucher, compliance with the published entry period, and staff's eligibility checks. A voucher is evidence for VRTon's process, **not independent payment verification**.
5. **Prizes are contributed by participating creators or partners.** The winners are selected using a **third-party random-selection website/service**; VRTon will announce the selection method and applicable rules before conducting the draw. Specific prizes, participating regions, entry opening/closing dates, currency-conversion method, notification/claim instructions, redraw rules, and any legal limits **must be published separately before the raffle opens**.
6. Prize contributors remain responsible for having the rights to deliver their promised items. VRTon will assist with coordination where practical but cannot guarantee third-party services or items beyond obligations imposed by applicable law or a separate written commitment.
7. **The raffle must not open or run if the necessary legal permissions, eligibility conditions, and published specific rules are absent.** VRTon may suspend or cancel an unlawful or impracticable raffle; donations already made to the charity are not ticket purchases paid to VRTon and are not refundable by VRTon.

## Article 13: Volunteers, status, and participation {#article-13-volunteers-status-and-participation}

1. Volunteering is optional and, **unless a separate written paid-services agreement says otherwise**, unpaid. Volunteer participation does not itself promise salary, remuneration, future employment, or permanent appointment. Separate paid projects are governed by their own agreements and applicable law.
2. Volunteer eligibility follows Article 3. Volunteers may propose responsibilities aligned with their skills and availability, decline tasks outside agreed responsibilities, and stop volunteering. Reasonable advance notice of withdrawal or missed deadlines is encouraged where possible so the team can arrange coverage.
3. Volunteers must respect the Code of Conduct, communicate responsibly, protect confidential work materials and personal information, follow relevant production/security instructions, and avoid misleading use of their position.
4. Volunteers are entitled to respectful treatment, fair consideration of safety concerns, and freedom from retaliation for good-faith reporting. They should not be required to disclose private health or personal circumstances merely to explain an absence.
5. VRTon may reassign roles, revoke staff access, or end a collaboration because of serious misconduct, inability to fulfil essential responsibilities, prolonged uncommunicated inactivity, or other reasonable organizational needs, subject to applicable law and Article 8’s moderation appeal provisions where a moderation action is imposed.
6. The [Volunteering Guide](/legal/volunteering/) provides a concise practical overview; it does not supersede these Terms.

## Article 14: Teams, leadership, and activity commitments {#article-14-teams-leadership-and-activity-commitments}

1. Volunteers are **normally encouraged to join no more than two work teams at once**, with exceptions approved by organizers when justified. Each team has a leader appointed by the Organizing Team, serving as the principal operational contact. An individual may lead **only one team at a time**.
2. VRTon's official Discord is the main volunteer coordination venue. Volunteers should monitor relevant work channels and communicate reasonably when they cannot meet a commitment, without implying an obligation to remain online continuously.
3. Concerns involving a team leader may be raised with the Organizing Team; concerns involving an organizer should be directed to an alternative authorized organizer or the official contact address rather than to the subject of the complaint.
4. Partner communities must designate a representative, coordinate agreed activities, and follow schedules, technical rules, and branding limits. Independent partner status does not give authority to bind VRTon to agreements, expenses, representations, or promises.
5. **DJs, singers, musicians, performing artists, presenters, and similar scheduled contributors** who accept a performance slot must also follow the [Performer Guidelines](/legal/performer-guidelines/). The confirmed booking, application conditions, technical brief, and any expressly accepted separate agreement may establish additional event-specific details; these must be communicated before acceptance.
6. **Performance slots:** Standard advertised musical slots are **45–55 minutes**, unless the parties expressly agree otherwise. Scheduling is negotiated with the performer, with reasonable consideration given to performers who have limited availability, but bookings and showtimes may change or be canceled for event or production reasons. Slots may be live or prerecorded.
7. **Live performance readiness:** Live performers must be available at least **15 minutes before their scheduled slot** for transmission and sound checks. They must have equipment and technical ability suitable for transmission, use the transmission method or services designated by VRTon (including any limits on bitrate, resolution, or related parameters), and attend reasonable pre-event technical tests offered by VRTon. Private Twitch, YouTube, or other stream links are not substitutes for the official transmission route unless the Organizing Team expressly approves. VRTon may cancel or replace a performance when there are reasonable grounds to believe the required technical standard cannot be met.
8. **Changes and promotion:** Performers should report inability to attend or deliver a prerecorded set as early as possible. VRTon may prioritize a performer who helps arrange a suitable replacement, without promising priority in any particular edition. Performers may promote their own artist profiles/social media during their appearance but must obtain prior approval to advertise paid products, outside communities, independent fundraisers, or other offers.
9. **On-stage content and promotion:** Performances must remain appropriate to VRTon’s SFW environment and the rules of the platforms involved. Material that is excessively explicit or materially disruptive may be limited or removed by staff. Performer avatars follow the same content and safety rules as other attendees, but production may approve less-optimized avatars for a scheduled performance.
10. **Artist materials and recordings:** VRTon may request an artist name, logo, visuals, and other promotional materials. The amount and timeliness of material supplied may affect how extensively the performance can be promoted; individual advertising is not guaranteed. Performers may share lawful recordings of their own appearances, subject to third-party rights and VRTon brand restrictions under Articles 15–17. VRTon may broadcast and reuse authorized event media under Article 5.

## Article 18: Privacy, personal data, and information requests {#article-18-privacy-personal-data-and-information-requests}

1. VRTon processes information necessary for event coordination, volunteer applications, donation-voucher accounting, permitted media, and safety operations, as described in the separate [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/).
2. Voucher submissions are restricted to the Directive/Organizing Team responsible for that function. Moderation reports are handled by the moderation team. Access to applications and volunteer coordination data is limited to authorized personnel who need it for their role.
3. VRTon currently does **not** have a fixed automatic deletion schedule for all records; some may be preserved for ongoing services, historical accountability, and transparency. This **does not override legal storage-limitation requirements** or individual rights. Information must be reviewed, erased, restricted, or de-identified where applicable law requires or where it is no longer necessary for a lawful stated purpose.
4. Requests for access, correction, deletion, and other available rights may be sent to **support@vrton.org**. Requests are assessed individually; deletion is not universally guaranteed, particularly for necessary legal records, safety evidence, already-distributed media, or information controlled by independent platforms, but statutory rights and deadlines will be honored.
5. VRTon does not operate or control the personal-data practices of independent payment processors or partner fundraising services.

## Article 19: Availability, technical risks, and responsibility {#article-19-availability-technical-risks-and-responsibility}

2. VRTon does not guarantee uninterrupted services, specific instance capacity, compatibility with all hardware, preservation of every broadcast, error-free software, or immunity from misconduct by other users.
3. Participants remain responsible for their own equipment, accounts, conduct, expenses, and interactions. Independent partner communities remain responsible for their own fundraisers and contractual commitments under Article 11.
4. **Nothing in these Terms excludes liability, participant rights, privacy protections, or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.** VRTon will consider credible reports and take reasonable action within its authority; it cannot guarantee prevention of every incident.

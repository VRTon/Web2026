VRTon brings together communities, artists, volunteers, and visitors to support a charitable cause. Our expectation is simple: **help everyone enjoy the event safely and respectfully**.

This is a practical guide. The [Terms of Service](/legal/terms/) contain the complete, authoritative rules. If this guide and the Terms differ, the Terms govern, subject to applicable law.

## Respect others

- No harassment, hate speech, threats, intimidation, bullying, stalking, unwanted advances, or repeated contact after someone asks you to stop.
- Respect personal space, blocks, moderators, and other guests.
- Good-faith criticism, factual questions, and concerns about fundraising are welcome. Fabricated evidence, knowingly false allegations, impersonation, and campaigns of targeted harassment or sabotage are not.
- Keep political campaigning, religious proselytizing, and confrontational debates out of official event activities.

[Detailed rules: Terms Article 6](/legal/terms/#article-6-respect-safety-and-prohibited-content)

## Keep the event SFW and accessible

- No explicit nudity, erotic roleplay, sexual activity, extreme gore, or deliberately disturbing content.
- No mic spam, crashers, malicious clients, invasive shaders, disruptive particles, exploit abuse, or intentional interference with streams and activities.
- **Optimized avatars are strongly recommended.** VRTon provides public optimized avatars. An unoptimized avatar may be hidden from streams, photos, or recordings, even if permitted in the event.
- Follow reasonable staff instructions, including requests to adjust avatars, audio, or behavior.

[Content rules: Article 6](/legal/terms/#article-6-respect-safety-and-prohibited-content) · [Avatars: Article 7](/legal/terms/#article-7-avatars-accessibility-and-media-selection)

## Protect the community and the cause

- Never share another person's private information, post malicious links, scam people, impersonate staff, or use unauthorized donation links.
- VRTon only endorses charity donation channels it identifies as official. Partner-organized collections are independent of VRTon.
- Ask for authorization before advertising another community, fundraiser, service, or product in an official space.

[Platform and identity rules: Article 4](/legal/terms/#article-4-platform-compliance-and-official-spaces) · [Fundraising: Article 11](/legal/terms/#article-11-partner-fundraising-official-channels-and-non-endorsement)

## Broadcasting and admission

**Official VRChat event spaces may be filmed or broadcast at any time**, with no guaranteed off-camera event areas. Event-related Twitch/YouTube chats may appear on screen. Volunteer work channels and meetings may also be recorded with appropriate notice; Discord DMs and ordinary general channels are not routinely filmed.

VRTon event access is **generally 18+**. A narrow exception may apply to a **preapproved, supervised institutional visit by persons aged 13–17** during a designated session in a non-age-gated main instance, with guardian permission and compliance with VRChat's account requirements. This is not an open admission exception.

[Admission: Article 3](/legal/terms/#article-3-access-age-and-participation) · [Recordings: Article 5](/legal/terms/#article-5-event-broadcasts-recording-and-media-use) · [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/)

## If something goes wrong

Staff may warn, mute, remove, or ban a participant. Serious safety issues may require immediate action. **Every VRTon moderation action may be appealed** through the official Discord server. If you are banned from Discord, appeal via **[support@vrton.org](mailto:support@vrton.org)**. **Do not message individual moderators in DMs.** Use the official private reporting route for incident reports.
